Monash Health Guided Category 2 Presentations

← Back to Monash Health Prompt Protocols

  • <28 days: All babies <28 days regardless of presentation
  • Cardiac chest pain: Active cardiac chest pain on arrival
  • Febrile neutropenia: Any patient presenting that is potentially neutropenic
  • MET criteria: Vitals taken at triage that fit MET call criteria
  • Ovarian torsion: Acute onset of moderate to severe unilateral pelvic pain, with associated nausea and vomiting
  • Potential inguinal hernia in paediatrics: Swollen scrotum/groin pain/palpable lump
  • Section 232: All MH 232 patients or presenting with police escort
  • Stroke code: MASS, symptoms onset within 9 hours for adults and paediatrics
  • Testicular pain: Symptoms of scrotal pain of any nature including visual or palpable lump in the groin
