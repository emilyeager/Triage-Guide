🚑 Presenting complaint

PQRST pain assessment

  • Provoking/palliating features: mobilising, positional, post-eating, inspiration, expiration etc.
  • Quality: sharp, dull, pressure, tight, throbbing, stabbing etc.
  • Radiation: spread to back, shoulder, neck, left arm, jaw, abdomen etc.
  • Severity: rate 0-10, distress ie: clutching chest, bent over etc.
  • Time: time of onset, constant or intermittent, nature of onset ie: gradual or sudden

Associated symptoms: headache, vomiting, nausea, visual changes, SOB, clammy, fever, syncopal

📜 History

Cardiac history: previous AMI, stents, CAD, IHD, HTN, high cholesterol, etc.

Neurological history: previous CVA

Diabetes

Smoking

Family cardiac history

Recent illness or infection

🩺 Assessment

A: Patency

B: WOB/SOB, SpO2, RR, chest auscultation

C: BP, HR, pulses, CRT, skin temp, ECG

D: GCS

E: temperature, pain

🚩 Red flags

Ongoing severe chest pain

Pain not worse on breathing or mobilisation

Haemodynamic instability

Diaphoresis

Pallor

Known cardiac history
